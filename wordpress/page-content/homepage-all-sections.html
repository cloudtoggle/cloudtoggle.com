How we calculate this

  • Compute spend = servers × hourly cost × 730 hours in an average month. Savings are the share of the 168-hour week your servers are switched off.
  • Only compute is counted. Stopped servers stop accruing compute charges, but storage, IP addresses and some licences attached to them can still be billed, depending on your provider.
  • The suggested plan is the smallest one covering your server count; the plan price is subtracted for the net figure. Over 200 servers needs a custom plan.
  • This is an estimate based on your inputs, not a guarantee. If you already use reserved instances or savings plans, your real saving may be lower.

Security and control

Built so the right people hold the switch.

How Cloud Toggle connects to your cloud, and how you control who can use it.

IAM roles with an external ID

Connect AWS with an IAM role, the recommended method, protected by an external ID of at least 20 characters. Account keys are also supported.

Scoped Azure roles

Azure access is granted through an app registration with the Virtual Machine Contributor and Monitoring Contributor roles on the subscription you connect.

Features, not full account access

Let people turn servers on and off without giving them access to your whole AWS or Azure account.

Role-based users and teams

Owner, Administrator and User permissions, plus teams, so the right people can act when they need to.

Test before you connect

Every connection has a built-in test, so you know the credentials work before anything is scheduled.

You choose what is imported

Servers are fetched automatically by default, or turn that off and add only the ones you want managed.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which cloud platforms does Cloud Toggle support?

Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Microsoft Azure. Each plan includes a set number of cloud connections, so you can connect more than one account or subscription on Plus and Pro.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are up and running in minutes. You add a cloud connection, your servers are imported automatically, and you assign them a schedule. Step-by-step guides for AWS and Azure are in the help centre.

What permissions does Cloud Toggle need in my cloud account?

On AWS you create an IAM policy from the JSON shown in the setup wizard, then connect with an IAM role (recommended, with an external ID) or with account keys. On Azure you register an app and assign it the Virtual Machine Contributor and Monitoring Contributor roles on the subscription you want to manage. You can review exactly what is requested before you grant it.

Is it safe to switch servers off automatically?

Schedules only apply to the servers you choose to add to them, and any schedule can be overridden in seconds, so someone who needs a server out of hours can turn it on without waiting. Start with a development or test environment and widen from there.

Do my team members need access to our AWS or Azure account?

No. Cloud Toggle lets you share access to specific features, such as turning a server on or off, without giving people access to the entire cloud account. Admins create individual users and role-based teams.

How does the free trial work?

The Basic plan ($49/month) includes a 30-day free trial, with up to 15 servers, 1 team, 5 users and 1 cloud connection, so you can try Cloud Toggle on real infrastructure before you pay.

What if I need more than 200 servers or something custom?

Get in touch and we will put a custom plan together. You can also book a demo and walk through your setup with us first.
