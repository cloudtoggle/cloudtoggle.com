Features

Everything you need to switch off waste. Nothing you don't.

Scheduling

Daily and weekly schedules, down to the quarter hour.

Build a schedule once and apply it to as many servers as you need. Pick the time zone, choose a 15 or 60 minute resolution and click the hours you want servers running.

  • Daily and weekly schedules with precise timing
  • Time zone aware, so "9 to 6" means your 9 to 6
  • One schedule can cover many servers

Manual overrides

Plans change. Override in seconds.

Someone needs the test environment tonight? Turn the server on from your server list. Your schedule picks up again afterwards, with no cloud console and no support ticket.

  • Every server, in AWS and Azure, in one list
  • See state, schedule, cloud connection and operating system at a glance
  • Filter by state, cloud connection or location

Access control and teams

Share the switch, not the cloud account.

Give people access to specific features without letting them into your entire AWS or Azure account. Invite users, group them into role-based teams and decide who can do what.

  • Individual users and role-based teams
  • Owner, Administrator and User permissions
  • No need to hand out cloud console access

AWS and Azure in one place

Manage servers from both clouds in a single view and on one set of schedules.

No engineering skills needed

A simple interface so anyone on the team can cut waste in a few clicks.

Pricing that scales with you

A flat monthly plan, starting at $49, based on how many servers you manage.
